01 · GATHER

At 5:30am a scheduled job pulls about forty feeds: RBA, ABS, ABC, SBS, JavaScript Weekly, smol.ai and the Bureau of Meteorology. Everything arrives in a jumble.

02 · THINK

Claude removes duplicates, ranks stories against what I care about and writes each one in its own words. Every line points to a real article; links are built in code, never by the model.

03 · DELIVER

Stories leave one at a time, sorted by section, into the morning edition. The app caches it for the train, and the 4pm run tops it up.
